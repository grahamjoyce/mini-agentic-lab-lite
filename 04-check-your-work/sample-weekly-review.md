# Fernhill weekly review: weeks 14 Sep to 2 Nov 2026 (data to 8 Nov)

## Headline
**The podcast looks like the most undervalued channel, and women's sizing is quietly costing us.** The platform credits the podcast with about 1% of new customers. Customers put it at about 18%, and it stopped on 11 October. Re-run it as a measured test, and fix the fit guidance for women's 8 to 10.

## Scorecard (store figures, so highest-trust)

| Measure | Target | 8-week actual | Latest week (2 Nov) | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MER | 3.0 or more | about 3.10 | about 2.97 | Met overall, slipping |
| New customer cost | AUD 90 or less | about AUD 101 | about AUD 104 | Missed |
| Payback (estimate) | Margin plus 60-day repeat covers the cost | about AUD 92 per customer against AUD 101 | n/a | Probably missed by about AUD 9 |

- Total spend was about AUD 139,200. Store revenue was AUD 431,300 from 1,372 new customers.
- The payback figure is rough. It assumes AUD 118 AOV, 62% margin, and a repeat order the same size. It uses a weighted 60-day repeat rate of about 25% from the survey.
- Hitting AUD 90 would have taken about 175 more new customers over the eight weeks, or about 22 a week.

## Where the evidence agrees and disagrees

| Channel | Platform share of new customers | Customer-reported source | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Podcast | about 1% | about 18% | Platform far too low |
| TikTok | about 4% | about 13% | Platform too low |
| Meta (both) | about 33% | about 13% (Instagram or Facebook) | Platform too high |
| Google (all) | about 50% | about 11% | Platform far too high |
| Friend or family | 0% | about 24% | No channel, but the biggest source |
| Email | about 12% | not an option in the survey | Collects, does not create |

- **Podcast.** The platform sees only promo-code orders. The survey, 31 reviews, and 27 FERN15 tickets all point the other way. Podcast customers are also the best: AUD 129 first order and 34% repeat. Survey-implied, that is roughly 250 new customers, or about AUD 25 each. Even at half that, it would be about AUD 50. This is an estimate with survey bias. I lean on the survey and tickets over the platform.
- **TikTok.** Last-click return looks poor. But 18 reviews say "saw it on TikTok, then searched". Its customers are the weakest: AUD 98 first order and 14% repeat. That is a Test, not a Cut, because the two sources conflict.
- **Meta and Google.** The platforms take credit for demand that word of mouth and the podcast likely created.

## Recommendations

1. **Re-run the podcast as a four-week test at about AUD 1,170 a week, with a new promo code and a vanity URL.**
   - **Why:** It is about 18% of survey answers, with the highest order value and repeat rate. The 20% cap on shifts lets you take about AUD 600 from Performance Max and about AUD 550 from non-brand.
   - **Proposed T-2:**
     - Main metric: weekly store new customers.
     - Success: an average of 175 or more over the four weeks. The 12 Oct to 2 Nov average was 165.
     - Stop: below 165 after two weeks.
     - Check: podcast share in the survey.
   - **Caveat:** T-1 overlaps. Read both with that in mind.
   - **Confidence:** Medium.
   - **What would change my mind:** A new-code and survey count far below about 25 a week.
   - **Cost of being wrong:** Medium, about AUD 4,700 over four weeks.

2. **Add a size and fit guide for women's 8 to 10 and narrow shoulders, on the product page and in the ads.**
   - **Why:** There were 58 sizing tickets and 38 reviews on it. The returns rate is 14%, and about 61% of returns cite size. That is roughly 8.5% of orders, or about 39 a week (estimate).
   - **Confidence:** Medium.
   - **What would change my mind:** Store data showing size returns spread across all sizes.
   - **Cost of being wrong:** Low.

3. **Check the new Meta creative leads on warmth and drops or qualifies "ultralight". Judge T-1 on store new customers as well as platform cost.**
   - **Why:** 71 reviews praise warmth, and 26 say it is heavier than the ads suggest. Platform cost per new customer is AUD 94.6. T-1's AUD 80 target is a platform figure, and scaled it is nearer AUD 150 now.
   - **Confidence:** Medium.
   - **What would change my mind:** A platform win with no store lift would mean the claim is over-credited.
   - **Cost of being wrong:** Low.

4. **Get a brand and non-brand split for Performance Max, via brand exclusions or a search-category report.**
   - **Why:** It is 18% of spend. Return fell from 4.3 to 3.6 while spend rose about 10%. Orders were 88 in the latest week, its lowest.
   - **Confidence:** Low to Medium.
   - **What would change my mind:** A split showing it is mostly non-brand and converting.
   - **Cost of being wrong:** Low.

5. **Hold brand search, retargeting, email, and TikTok at current spend.**
   - **Why:** The first three mostly capture demand (L-2). TikTok's 0.67 return is last-click only, and survey and reviews conflict with it.
   - **Confidence:** High for the capture channels, Low for TikTok.
   - **What would change my mind:** A holdout test showing TikTok adds nothing. Run it after T-1 and T-2, not alongside them.
   - **Cost of being wrong:** Low.

## What we cannot tell yet
- Whether new Meta creative is live, and whether it works.
- How much of the post-11 October dip in new customers is the podcast ending, Meta fade, or launch decay. New customers fell from about 178 a week to about 165.
- Real repeat value by source. The survey is self-reported, and it covers 30% of customers.
- Why friend and family is the biggest source. Nothing in our spend feeds it directly. A referral test could come later.
