# Evidence templates

Blank files for your own brand, with the column headings the agent knows. Copy the ones you need into your brand's evidence folder and fill them in.

## Which files

Every brand needs at least one file of each kind:

- **Evidence A: what your systems report.**
  - Online sales (e-commerce, DTC): `evidence-a1-platform-performance.csv` **and** `evidence-a2-store-totals.csv`.
  - Leads and deals (B2B, services): `evidence-a1-leads-by-source.csv`.
- **Evidence B: what customers say.**
  - `evidence-b2-customer-voice.md`, and `evidence-b1-customer-survey.csv` if you ask "How did you first hear about us?"

If you have no customer voice at all, start one now: add a single question to your order confirmation or enquiry form, "How did you first hear about us?", with a fixed list of answers. It is the most useful evidence the agent gets.

## How to fill them in

- One row per week (per campaign, or per source). `week_start` is the Monday, written `2026-11-02`.
- Money is in your brand's currency, as plain numbers: `5426`, not `$5,426`.
- Platform figures are what the ad platform claims. Store figures are what your shop or CRM actually recorded. Keep them apart; the gap between them is the point.
- Use the `notes` column for anything odd: a tracking break, a one-off bulk order, a price change, a sale.
- Keep personal data out: no customer names, emails, or phone numbers.

## Column names

Keep the headings in these templates. The agent knows them, and they match the demo files. Other headings still work, but the agent then has to work out what each column means, so check its sums more closely.
