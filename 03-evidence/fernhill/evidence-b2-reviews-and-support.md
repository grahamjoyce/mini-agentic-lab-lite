# Evidence B2: Reviews and support themes

Fernhill Outdoor (fictional). Period: 14 September to 8 November 2026.
This file goes with `evidence-b1-customer-survey.csv`. Together they make up the customer voice evidence.

## How the survey was run

- One question on the order confirmation page: "How did you first hear about us?"
- Single choice from a fixed list. Customers could not pick more than one.
- 412 of 1,372 new customers answered. That is a 30% response rate.
- Customers who bought on a phone answered less often than customers on a laptop. We have not corrected for this.
- Groups under 30 respondents (YouTube or review site, Magazine or event) are too small to lean on.

## Creative in market during the period

- Meta prospecting: "Ridge Jacket. Ultralight. Ultra warm." One main video, two static variants. No new creative since launch on 14 September.
- TikTok creator whitelisting: three creators posting trail stories. The brand's link sits in each creator's bio. Promo code is not used.
- Podcast: host-read spot on Long Run Radio, four weeks, ending 11 October.
- Email: welcome flow (four emails) and a 60-day winback flow. Both unchanged since August.
