# Client brain: Fernhill Outdoor (demo client)

Client id: `fernhill`. This file holds the whole brain for this client. Add new reviews, tests, and learnings at the top of their section.

## Profile

- **Summary:** Direct-to-consumer trail running apparel brand in Australia. Hero product is the Ridge Jacket, launched 14 September 2026. Average order value is about AUD 118 and gross margin is 62%.
- **Customer:** Trail runners aged 28 to 50. They run with clubs and friends and trust people they know over adverts.
- **Positioning in market:** The Ridge Jacket: ultralight, ultra warm.
- **Channels:** Meta, Google, TikTok creators, Email, Podcast
- **Currency:** AUD
- **Constraints:** Total marketing spend is about AUD 17,000 a week. Do not move more than 20% of any channel's budget in one cycle.
- **Maximum budget shift per cycle (%):** 20
- **Measurement notes:** Platforms over-claim revenue against the store, so always compare with store totals. A post-purchase survey asks how new customers first heard of the brand. Promo code FERN15 was tied to the podcast sponsorship.
- **Review cadence:** Weekly review on Mondays.

## Targets

Judge every review against these.

- **MER (store revenue divided by total marketing spend):** at least 3.0
- **New customer cost (total marketing spend divided by store new customers):** no more than AUD 90
- **Payback:** First-order margin plus 60-day repeat purchases should cover the cost of winning the customer.

## Learnings

Things we know. Newest first. If something new contradicts an old line, mark the old one as replaced. Do not delete it.

- **L-3** (2026-11-02, Medium, podcast, promo-code) Some podcast listeners were still using promo code FERN15 after the sponsorship ended. [source: support tickets]
